If something breaks

No network

The app works offline after the first load; open it before the slot. The POC pages render from files in the site.

npx is slow

Run the terminal command once beforehand so the package is cached, or show evidence/ruflo-policy-legacy.json.

Questions about rUv's projects

Say "per its README" and point at the link. Star counts are snapshots. What we quote about RuVector and Ruflo is in their own words.

"Did you fake the policy admin?"

No. Ruflo's CLI needs an interactive terminal for policy admin; POC1 uses the library engine, which is its documented programmatic surface. POC6 is the interactive version, as a runbook.
