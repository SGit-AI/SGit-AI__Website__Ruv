It works like git: commit, branch, merge, push, clone.Each agent works on its own branch; the branches merge back into main. Timings are medians of real runs.
Why agents: a shared, versioned memory across machines and agents.A fresh clone anywhere gets the whole history back. Or: one vault per run, deleted at the end, and the delete verified.
