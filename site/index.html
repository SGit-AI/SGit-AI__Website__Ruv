Agent work by a Ruflo hive, with a written mandate per role and a vault you keep, delivered by people who do it in the open.

Each job is a ruv.sgit run: a queen and workers under an Agent Behaviour Policy, every step committed to an encrypted sgit vault that is handed to you at the end. Small steps at published prices. Enquire, and a person replies with dates and a quote.

Rules of engagement

The rules, in four lines

  • Open source.Our code is Apache-2.0 and our materials CC BY 4.0, so anybody can do this without us, and we encourage it.
  • Your code and data stay yours.Each job runs in its own encrypted vault, handed to you at the end. Client work is private first.
  • An equal split.Revenue is split equally between the parties, after direct costs are reimbursed to whoever paid them.
  • Whoever has the client bills.If you bring the client, you invoice and pay the other parties their share.
