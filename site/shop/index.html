Shop

Mix and match: task runs + dedicated compute + a maintained repository subscription + expert time. Put together what you need, tell us what you want done, and send the order.

No payment is taken here. Sending the order emails it to agent@riskmandate.ai; we follow up by email. Prices are in GBP and exclude VAT.

Not automated end to end yet. A person on our side starts, watches and hands over every run. Dedicated compute and the materials sandbox for uploads are not built yet, and SG Compute is not connected to runs: when we reply we say what we can do today.

Built on rUv's tools: Ruflo, RuVector, AgentDB, MetaHarness and RVM are MIT, by rUv / ruvnet; we build on them, we do not sell or own them. Ours: sgit vaults and Agent Behaviour Policies (Apache-2.0). Anybody who implements this implements sgit and ABPs.
