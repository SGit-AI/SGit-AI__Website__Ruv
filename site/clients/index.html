What stays yours

Everything we publish is open, so anybody can do this without us. Your part of an engagement is not.

Your code and data

They stay yours, under your own terms. We do not publish them, reuse them or keep them beyond the engagement.

The vault

Each job runs in its own encrypted sgit vault: a branch per worker, every delivery committed. At the end it is handed to you, as a zip or with a read key only you choose to publish, and deleted from our side, with the delete checked.

Private first

Everything to do with a client is private by default. A ledger entry or a case study is published later, and only with your agreement; then only the measurements.

What goes back

Improvements to the shared code, playbooks and behaviour-policy patterns go back to the public repositories, with your name and data removed. Code created in an engagement is Apache-2.0 unless agreed otherwise in writing.

The full terms, with worked examples of who bills and how revenue is split, are in the rules of engagement.

For platform, AI enablement, security and risk teams

Agents inside your organisation, with the governance written down

People across the organisation want agents doing real work. Security, risk and legal want to know what those agents can touch, before they say yes. This gives both.

A written mandate per role

An Agent Behaviour Policy for each role: what it can reach, what it is mandated to do, the barrier between the two, and the person who owns it.

Enforced, not advised

The mandate compiles into Ruflo's own policy engine in enforce mode (on a fresh project it runs in legacy mode; observe sits between). Ruflo writes a hash-chained receipt for every decision. Reading data/report.json… POC1

No network by default

Runs get no internet. Materials are provided up front, and the policy denies egress.

Data with a short life

Files are encrypted on the client before upload; the server stores ciphertext under opaque ids. One vault per run holds everything it made; at the close it is measured, handed over and deleted, and the delete is checked.

Where it runs

  • In your own environment: the maintained repository, kept current and set up for your stack, on Claude Code, on AWS or anywhere you run code.
  • On its own machine: dedicated compute, one AWS EC2 instance per task, with the vault created inside it. Designed, not built yet.

How a provider helps you start

  • A couple of hours to review your first policy.
  • A day to run the first hive with your team.
  • Four days to set up your deployment and hand it over.
