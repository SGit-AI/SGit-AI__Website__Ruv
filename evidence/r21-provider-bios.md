### Draft bio, long (~180 words, third person)

Dinis Cruz builds open-source tools, companies and communities that help organisations understand, manage and improve complex systems. He has more than 20 years of experience across cyber security and software engineering, including roles as CISO and CTO.

He is building sgit (sgit.ai), the encrypted git for humans and AI agents: files are encrypted on the client and the server stores only ciphertext. It is the foundation for RiskMandate.ai, VoiceDebrief.ai and The Cyber Boardroom, and SGraph AI commercialises it as SG/Send and SG/Vaults. He founded The Cyber Boardroom, which uses generative AI to help boards and executives understand cyber security.

In the OWASP community, he is a former Board member. He created the O2 Platform and the OSBot / MGraph-DB family of open-source tools, and organised the OWASP Summits (Lisbon 2011, Woburn 2017) that became the Open Security Summit. He has written ten books on Leanpub, including "Generation Z Developers" and "SecDevOps Risk Workflow". He speaks regularly on GenAI and security; most recently he gave the first community talk at Agentic Engineering Live in London (October 2026) for the Agentics Foundation's London chapter. He is based in London.

Mondweep Chakravorty is an enterprise AI leader and agentic systems builder based in London. Since September 2024 he has worked as an independent AI advisor through DxSure Ltd. He co-founded the Agentics Foundation's London chapter, which runs the city's agentic AI meetups, and mentors on the Johns Hopkins Agentic AI programme. At the CISO London Summit 2025 he co-presented the keynote "Securing the Agentic Frontier", applying the OWASP MAESTRO threat-modelling framework to an agentic AI codebase. He builds in public through his "vibe-cast" lab on GitHub. He contributed to Ruflo and ran the first SGit × Ruflo proof of concept.
